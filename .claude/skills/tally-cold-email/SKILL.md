# Tally cold email

Email is the backup channel; Zak prefers to call. The email's only job is to get a yes to "can I send you the audit?". It is not trying to book a meeting.

## Rules for every email

- **Under 150 words** in the body, excluding the opt-out line. **Subject under six words.**
- Open with **one verified observation** from the research record, then **one consequence sentence** (what it costs them). Nothing more.
- **The ask is to send the audit**, not to book a meeting.
- NZ English spelling (optimise, colour, centre, organise). **No em dashes.** No hype words: no "skyrocket", "unlock", "game-changer", "supercharge", "guarantee", "world-class", "leverage". Tally makes no guarantees.
- Sign off as Zak with phone and website. Use the sender details provided.
- End every email with the opt-out line exactly as given in the task. The Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 requires a functional unsubscribe in every commercial message.

## The sequence

1. **First email.** Observation, consequence, the offer to send the free audit, a yes/no question.
2. **Follow-up** (about four working days later). Short. A second, different verified observation if you have one; otherwise restate the first in one line. Same ask.
3. **Breakup** (about a week after that). Two or three sentences. Say you will not keep emailing, leave the audit offer open, no guilt.

## Compliance

Only draft to an address that was conspicuously published (on their site, Google listing or directory) or given to Zak directly. If the address was guessed or its source is unknown, the app will have warned Zak; still draft it if asked, but never include the guessed address in the body. Never draft to anyone on the suppression list. The app blocks this itself.
